Goal:
Create a premium fantasy football SOS tool with two main views:

Team SOS
Player SOS

The tool should support:

Season selector, default 2026
Position filter: QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DST
Scoring filter: PPR, Half-PPR, Standard
View filter: Full Season, Rest of Season, Playoffs
Metric filter: Opponent, Rank, Adjusted Fantasy Points Allowed, Difficulty Score
Download CSV button

Data model:
Create or use tables/data files for:

nfl_schedules: season, week, team, opponent, is_home, game_date, bye
fantasy_points_allowed: season, week, defense_team, position, scoring_format, raw_points_allowed, adjusted_points_allowed, rank, difficulty_bucket
team_sos: season, team, position, scoring_format, weeks 1-18, po2, po3, ros
player_sos: season, player_id, player_name, team, position, weeks 1-18, po2, po3, ros

For 2026 preseason:
Use the released 2026 NFL schedule and apply 2025 adjusted fantasy points allowed as the baseline difficulty model. Once 2026 games begin, blend 2026 data into the model.

Difficulty buckets:

Rank 1-5: Very Tough
Rank 6-10: Tough
Rank 11-22: Neutral
Rank 23-27: Favorable
Rank 28-32: Smash Spot

Team SOS table:
Rows should be NFL teams. Columns should be W1-W18 plus PO2, PO3, and ROS. Each weekly cell should show opponent and either adjusted points allowed, rank, or difficulty depending on selected metric.

Player SOS table:
Rows should be fantasy players. Columns should include Player, Team, Position, SOS Rank, Playoff SOS, ROS SOS, and weekly opponent cells.

UI/UX:
Use StatChasers branding. Dark background, clean card containers, gold accent lines, subtle shadows, rounded corners, sticky table headers, sticky first column, and mobile horizontal scrolling. Do not copy competitor wording or design. Make the page feel like a premium StatChasers tool.

Add top insight cards:

Best QB Playoff Schedule
Worst RB Playoff Schedule
Best ROS Schedule
Toughest Opening Stretch

Add methodology section:
Explain that SOS is based on adjusted fantasy points allowed by position, removing schedule bias so teams are compared more fairly.

Add SEO:
H1: Fantasy Football Strength of Schedule
Meta title: Fantasy Football Strength of Schedule | 2026 SOS by Team & Player
Meta description: View 2026 fantasy football strength of schedule by team, player, position, scoring format, rest-of-season, and fantasy playoff weeks.
